### Description
**Dawaa+** is your smart health companion for managing medications and medical appointments effortlessly. Full Arabic and English support.

**Key Features:**

💊 **Smart Medication Management**
- Add medications with dosage, form, and photo
- Flexible scheduling (once, multiple times daily, or as-needed)
- Inventory tracking with low-stock alerts

⏰ **Precise Dose Reminders**
- Local notifications work even when the app is closed
- Voice alerts for each dose
- Daily summary in a single notification
- 30-minute grace period before a dose is marked missed

📊 **Interactive Dose History**
- View your dose history day by day
- **New:** Toggle any dose (Taken ↔ Missed) for any past day
- Weekly and monthly adherence statistics

❤️ **Blood Pressure Tracking**
- Log readings easily
- Clear trend charts
- PDF reports ready to print or share with your doctor

🧪 **Lab Tests**
- Manual entry with reference ranges
- Export formatted PDF reports
- Interactive fullscreen preview

📅 **Medical Appointments**
- Organize doctor visits
- Pre-appointment reminders

🌍 **Full Arabic Support**
- Native RTL interface
- Switch between Arabic and English in Settings

🔐 **Privacy First**
- Guest mode works without sign-up
- Secure cloud sync when logged in
- Full account & data deletion anytime

📤 **Improved Sharing**
- Share the app with friends as a link or via social platforms (WhatsApp, Telegram, Email)

## 📸 Screenshots Required (App Store Connect)

You must provide screenshots at these exact sizes. We generate two ready-to-upload sets automatically:

| Device | Resolution | Generated by script | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| iPhone 6.5" (XS Max / 11 Pro Max) | **1242 × 2688** | ✅ `seed-and-shoot-appstore.mjs` | Upload as 6.5" set; Apple can scale to 6.7"/6.9" |
| iPad Pro 12.9" (2nd–6th gen) | **2048 × 2732** | ✅ `seed-and-shoot-appstore.mjs` | Upload as 12.9" set |

**Suggested screen flow (7 screenshots per device):**
1. الصفحة الرئيسية مع ملخص الجرعات (Home + today's dose summary)
2. قائمة الأدوية مع المخزون (Medications)
3. سجل الجرعات (History / dose adherence)
4. ضغط الدم — الرسم البياني (Blood Pressure)
5. التحاليل المخبرية (Lab Tests)
6. المواعيد الطبية (Appointments)
7. الإعدادات + مشاركة التطبيق (Settings + Share)

**How to generate automatically:**
```bash
npm install   # if not already done
node seed-and-shoot-appstore.mjs
```
Screenshots are saved to `/mnt/documents/appstore-screenshots/iphone-6.5/` and `/mnt/documents/appstore-screenshots/ipad-12.9/`.

**How to capture manually (official Apple way):**
1. Xcode → Simulator → choose iPhone 16 Pro Max or iPad Pro 12.9".
2. Run the app: `npx cap run ios`.
3. For each screen press `Cmd+S` to save a screenshot at the official resolution.
